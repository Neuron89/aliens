## PLAUSIBLE (not verified)

- Room names (including GM renames of unrevealed rooms) reach the TV/phones (`board.js:136, 269-273`) — spoiler risk depending on fog rendering.
- No size cap on `send-message` / `broadcast-message` text or `priority` type (`:659, :747`); snapshot bloat.
- `get-assigned-characters` (`:735`) has no auth; `assignedCharacterIds` can drift when one character is assigned to two seats.
- `end-game` leaves players in the socket room with stale `socket.data`; `lastSavedEmit` not cleared.
- Client outbox flushes on connect before `rejoin-game` completes; retried not duplicated, but `applyRestore` (`GameContext.jsx:223`) hides queued messages until confirmed.
- Content and generate HTTP APIs are unauthenticated on the LAN (overwrite/delete packs, start paid generations).

## Checked and fine

Role filtering (`viewFor`, `tableView`) strips hidden tokens, notes, unrevealed-room tokens, GM-only clocks and pause identity. GM handlers go through `dmOf`; player handlers derive the player from the socket; board clients cannot mutate. Snapshot file names come only from server codes validated against `^[A-Z]{4}$`; content ids are strictly validated; writes are debounced and atomic and flushed on exit. Generator aborts on client disconnect and always decrements its concurrency counter. No listener accumulation across `GameProvider` re-mounts; RUN view clamps indices.
